The Mathematics Behind the 24 Game

This game develops number sense, order of operations, inverse operations and flexible mathematical thinking. Different expressions can give the same result, and brackets can completely change the value.

How does the computer check your answer?

The program parses your expression according to the usual order of operations and verifies that the four displayed numbers are used exactly once. It computes with fractions, so a result such as 23.999999 is never mistaken for 24.

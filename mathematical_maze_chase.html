How to play

Move with the arrow keys or WASD. Collect all yellow dots to complete the maze. Numbers that are even numbers are forbidden; other numbers are safe to eat for 5 bonus points. All numbers look alike, so check before moving!

Press T to teleport to a safe, open tile as often as needed. When 25% or fewer dots remain, teleportation favours places near remaining dots; with five or fewer, it brings you very close. Release the arrow key to stop moving.

Each forbidden number or gremlin collision costs a life. Safe numbers are optional and do not count towards the dots remaining. When you lose a life, you return to a safe starting position. Collecting all dots advances to the next, faster level.

Controls

P pauses or resumes. The game is playable with a keyboard or the on-screen direction buttons. Gremlins use different pursuit and patrol strategies.

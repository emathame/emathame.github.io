Set up your game

Press an arrow key or WASD to start moving and steer. On touchscreens, use the direction buttons. Press Space to pause or resume. The snake gets faster and the collection target increases after every completed screen. Earn one bonus life after completing every third level (maximum five lives). Changing the number category automatically starts a new game, so the mission always matches your selection.

Mathematical guide

Which numbers should I collect?

Even: divisible by 2. Odd: not divisible by 2. Prime: greater than 1 with exactly two positive divisors. Composite: greater than 1 and not prime. Multiples: divisible by the chosen number. Square: 1, 4, 9, 16, 25, … . Triangular: 1, 3, 6, 10, 15, … .

Remember: 1 is neither prime nor composite.

How does the game choose the numbers?

Numbers are selected at random within the level's range. Each fresh screen contains correct and incorrect targets, including at least one correct target. Eating a correct number grows the snake by one segment and adds to your score. Eating a wrong number costs a life.

How do levels progress?

Start with five numbers to collect. Each completed screen increases the number required by two and speeds up the snake. More targets appear on the board as you advance. You keep your score and remaining lives. Completing levels 3, 6, 9 and so on awards one extra life, up to a maximum of five. Each new level waits for your first arrow key. There is no difficulty selection.

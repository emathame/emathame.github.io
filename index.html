Welcome

A little curiosity goes a long way

I'm George Dimitriadis, a retired mathematics teacher and lifelong enthusiast of puzzles, storytelling and the unexpected side of everyday life.

Here you'll find mathematical curiosities, classroom humour, original stories, reflections and educational resources. Whether you're a student, a teacher or simply someone who enjoys a good question, I hope you'll find something to make you think — and occasionally smile.

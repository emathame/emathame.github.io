Welcome to PhiloMathematica

Welcome! I'm George Dimitriadis, a retired mathematics teacher and lifelong enthusiast of puzzles, storytelling and the unexpected side of everyday life.

Here you'll find mathematical curiosities, classroom humour, educational resources, original stories and reflections on the world around us.

Whether you're a student, a teacher or simply someone who enjoys asking questions, I hope you'll find something here to make you think — and occasionally smile.

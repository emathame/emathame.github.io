Click or tap a line between adjacent dots. Each completed square earns one point and another turn. A line that completes two squares earns two points. The player with the most squares wins. The computer’s new line flashes yellow before its next move. Challenge mode uses chain-aware tactics and exact endgame calculation when few lines remain. Choose New Game to change settings.

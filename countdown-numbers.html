The Mathematics Behind Countdown

This game explores mental arithmetic, factors, multiples, inverse operations and strategic estimation. Often it is useful to build a large product, then adjust it by adding or subtracting smaller amounts.

How does the computer check your answer?

The program checks that each number used appears among the six displayed numbers and is not used more often than it appears. It then evaluates the expression using the standard order of operations and exact fractions. You may leave unused numbers aside.

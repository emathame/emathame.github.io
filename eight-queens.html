The Mathematics Behind This Puzzle

The Eight Queens Puzzle asks us to place eight queens on a chessboard so that no two queens attack one another.

Since a queen can move horizontally, vertically and diagonally, a valid arrangement must contain exactly one queen in each row and each column, with no two queens sharing a diagonal.

Why are there 92 solutions?

If we place exactly one queen in each row and column, there are initially 8! = 40,320 possible arrangements.

However, most of these arrangements contain queens that attack one another diagonally. Eliminating these leaves exactly 92 valid solutions.

Why only 12 fundamental solutions?

Some solutions can be obtained from others by rotating or reflecting the chessboard.

A square has eight possible symmetry operations: four rotations (including the identity) and four reflections.

When solutions related by these operations are considered equivalent, the 92 arrangements fall into just 12 groups, called fundamental solutions.

Eleven fundamental solutions generate eight distinct arrangements each. One generates only four because it has a rotational symmetry.

Thus, 11 × 8 + 1 × 4 = 92.

A mathematical challenge

Can you find the solution that remains unchanged when the chessboard is rotated through 180 degrees?

Hint: Among the 12 fundamental solutions, exactly one has this property.

Show Explanation

Of the 12 fundamental Eight Queens solutions, exactly one is unchanged by a rotation of 180 degrees.

Its queens occupy the following columns, reading from the top row to the bottom row:

3, 5, 2, 8, 1, 7, 4, 6

Notice that the columns in the bottom four rows are the reverse complements of those in the top four rows. Rotating the board through 180 degrees therefore leaves the arrangement unchanged.

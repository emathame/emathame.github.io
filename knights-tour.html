Knight's Tour

Move the knight so that it visits every square exactly once. A knight moves two squares in one direction and one square at right angles.

Squares visited: 0 / 64Remaining: 64
Choose any square to place the knight and begin.
Green dots indicate legal moves. Numbers mark previously visited squares. A hint suggests a move that can lead to a complete tour when one is found.

A tour that visits every square is called an open tour. The knight does not have to return to its starting square.
